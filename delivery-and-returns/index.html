Delivery

We deliver anywhere in South Africa by courier. We don't have a shop or collection counter.

  • The courier cost is worked out for your address and shown on your quote, before you pay.
  • In-stock items are dispatched within [X] working days after your payment clears.
  • Items marked "Available to order" take the lead time shown on the product.
  • The goods become your responsibility once they are delivered.

Check your delivery

Please check the goods when they arrive. Tell us about any damage, missing items or wrong parts within [X] working days by emailing sales@renco.za.net or calling Nicole on 021 572 7777.

Returning goods

You can cancel an order or return goods within 7 days of receiving them, without giving a reason, if the goods are:

  • unused
  • in their original packaging
  • in the same condition as when you received them.

We may refuse a return that doesn't meet these conditions.

Handling charge: a 15% handling charge applies to accepted change-of-mind returns. This does not apply to the cases in the box below.

Return costs: you pay the cost and risk of sending goods back to us. We recommend a trackable, insured courier, because we can't be responsible for goods lost or damaged on the way back. We may ask for proof that you sent them.

Different rules: faulty goods, individuals and sole proprietors

Faulty, unsafe or not as described: if you are covered by the Consumer Protection Act, you can return these within 6 months for a repair, replacement or refund. There is no handling charge, and we pay the return costs.

Individuals and sole proprietors buying online: you can cancel within 7 days of receiving the goods under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act. You pay only the direct cost of returning them. There is no handling charge, and we refund you within 30 days.

Nothing here limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or ECTA.

Items we can't take back

Unless they are faulty, or the law says otherwise:

  • special-order or custom-made goods
  • goods that deteriorate or expire quickly
  • sealed goods opened after delivery, where they can't be returned for health or safety reasons
  • goods that have been fitted or mixed with other items
  • sale and clearance items.

Refunds

When the goods reach us, we check them and let you know whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are paid within 14 days (30 days for cooling-off cancellations, as above), to the account you paid from.

How to return something

  1. 1Email sales@renco.za.net with your order reference (for example RE-261008-7Q4K) and what you want to return.
  2. 2We reply with the return address and confirm whether a handling charge applies.
  3. 3Send the goods in their original packaging and keep your proof of sending.
