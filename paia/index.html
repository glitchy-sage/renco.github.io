6. Processing of personal information (POPIA)

Purposes. Answer enquiries, quote and supply goods, keep tax records, and keep the website secure.

Categories of people. Customers and their staff, suppliers, and people who contact us.

Recipients. Cloudflare and Resend (USA), our email provider [EMAIL HOST], and couriers and banks for offline orders [TO BE CONFIRMED BY THE OWNER].

Transfers outside South Africa. Cloudflare and Resend process your message in the United States. We have accepted their data processing agreements, which require them to keep your information secure and to use it only to provide their service to us. POPIA section 72 allows a transfer like this when the recipient is bound by an agreement that gives adequate protection.

Security measures. Your message travels over an encrypted connection. It isn't stored on our website. Only our staff can open our inbox, and our accounts use two-factor authentication. We use a spam check and rate limits to block abuse.
