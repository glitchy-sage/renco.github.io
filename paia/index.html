2. The Information Regulator's guide

The Information Regulator publishes a guide on how to use the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA). You can find it, and the Regulator's contact details, at inforegulator.org.za.

6. Processing of personal information (POPIA)

Purposes. To quote, invoice and deliver orders, answer your questions, keep the tax records the law requires, and keep the website secure.

Categories of people. Customers and their staff contacts, suppliers, and people who visit the website or contact us.

Who we share it with. Courier companies, our card payment provider when you pay by card link, our email provider (Resend), and our website host (Cloudflare).

Transfers outside South Africa. Cloudflare and Resend process some information outside South Africa, including in the USA, under their data processing terms.

Security measures. Encrypted connections, a bot check and rate limits, a single protected email inbox that only our staff can open, and two-factor authentication on our accounts.
