This page explains what this website stores in your browser, and why. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

1. What we use

This website stores only the items in this table.

NameWhere it is storedWhat it doesConsent needed?
renco-cart-v1Your browser (local storage)Remembers the lines in your order request.No
Cloudflare security cookies (for example __cf_bm and cf_clearance)Cookies set by Cloudflare, our website hostProtects the site against bots and abuse.No
Cloudflare TurnstileOrder request page onlyChecks that a real person sends the order request, to stop spam.No
Cloudflare Web AnalyticsNothing is stored. It uses no cookies.Counts page views in total. It holds no personal information.No

2. Why none of these need your consent

The first three items are strictly necessary. They keep your order and the site working and safe. The last item uses no cookies and no personal information. Because of this, we do not show a cookie banner.

3. What we do not use

We do not use:

  • advertising cookies;
  • tracking cookies or analytics cookies that follow you between websites;
  • social media cookies;
  • embedded videos or maps that set cookies.

4. How to clear them

Most browsers let you delete cookies and site data. Look for Settings, then Privacy or Site settings, and find this website.

Clearing the site data for this website also removes your saved order. You can rebuild it from the products pages.
