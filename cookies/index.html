2. What we do not use

We don't use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, social media cookies, or embedded videos or maps. Because of this, we don't show a cookie banner.

3. How to clear them

Most browsers let you delete cookies and site data. Look for Settings, then Privacy or Site settings, and find this website.
