We collect only what we need to process and deliver your order. We don't sell your information, send marketing emails or use tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

Renco Electrical CC (registration no. [CK REG NO]), [LEGAL / REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Website: renco.za.net.

Our Information Officer under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is Nicole Du Toit. Phone [CELL NUMBER], email nicole@renco.za.net.

2. What we collect

  • When you send an order request: company name, contact person, email, phone, delivery address, and optionally your VAT number, order or PO number and delivery notes. You must give the required details for us to process your order.
  • Your order (cart): kept only in your own browser until you send it. It holds product numbers and quantities, nothing personal.
  • Security: your IP address is processed briefly by our hosting provider and its bot check (Cloudflare and Cloudflare Turnstile) to block spam and attacks.
  • Site statistics: anonymous page-view counts with no cookies and no personal information.

We never ask for ID numbers, dates of birth or card details. Card payments happen on our payment provider's own secure page.

3. Why we use it

To quote, invoice and deliver your order, to answer your questions, and to keep the tax records the law requires.

Our legal basis is that we need your information to carry out the contract you ask us to make, and to meet our legal duties. We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not send newsletters.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run your order:

  • our courier companies, who need your delivery address and phone number;
  • our card payment provider, if you choose to pay by card link;
  • our email provider (Resend), which sends our order emails;
  • our website host (Cloudflare).

Cloudflare and Resend process information outside South Africa, including in the USA, under their data processing terms. POPIA section 72 allows this where the information stays protected to a similar standard.

We do not sell your information.

5. How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for 5 years, as the Tax Administration Act requires, and are then deleted.

6. How we keep it safe

This website has no accounts and no database. Your order request travels over an encrypted connection to a single email inbox that only our staff can open. We use a bot check and rate limits to block abuse, and we keep our system accounts protected with two-factor authentication.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it (where the law allows), or object to how we use it.

Contact Nicole Du Toit on [CELL NUMBER] or nicole@renco.za.net.

8. Complaints

Please talk to us first so we can try to put things right.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za. [Complaints email address as published by the Regulator, to be checked before launch.]

9. Cookies

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. See our cookie policy for what the site stores in your browser.
