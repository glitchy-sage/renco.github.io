We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry. You contact us by phone or email; there is no contact form on this website. We don't sell your information, send marketing or use tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

Renco Electrical CC (registration no. [CK REG NO]), [POSTAL ADDRESS]. Website: renco.za.net.

2. Information Officer

Our Information Officer under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is Nicole, the Information Officer of Renco Electrical CC. Phone 021 572 7777, email nicole@renco.za.net.

3. What we collect

When you email us: your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you write or attach (for example a list of parts).

When you phone us: your name, number and what you need.

Our website host, 1-grid, keeps standard server logs (your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser type) to run and secure the website. We don't use these logs to identify you.

Please don't send ID numbers, bank details or health information.

4. Why, and our lawful basis

To answer your enquiry and, if you ask, to prepare a quote. We rely on POPIA section 11(1)(b) (steps you asked us to take before a possible contract) and section 11(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in replying to messages sent to us). Sharing your details is voluntary, but we can't reply without a way to contact you. We never use your enquiry for marketing and never add you to a mailing list.

5. Who receives it

1-grid [ATTORNEY/owner: exact company name from 1-grid's terms], a South African company, hosts our website and our email, so your email is stored in our mailbox with them. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else for their own use. Your own email provider handles your message on its way to us; that is between you and them.

Our website and email are hosted in South Africa.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails and phone notes for 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If your enquiry leads to a sale, quotes and invoices are kept for 5 years, as the Tax Administration Act requires. 1-grid keeps server logs for [PERIOD, to be confirmed].

7. How we keep it safe

Our website has no forms and stores nothing you type. Our mailbox is password protected. Our website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Ordinary email is not always encrypted between providers, so please don't email sensitive details.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact Nicole on 021 572 7777 or nicole@renco.za.net.

9. Complaints

Please talk to us first. You may also complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, 010 023 5200.

10. Cookies

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. See our cookie policy.
