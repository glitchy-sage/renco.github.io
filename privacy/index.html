We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry. We don't sell your information, send marketing or use tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

Renco Electrical CC (registration no. [CK REG NO]), [POSTAL ADDRESS]. Website: renco.za.net.

2. Information Officer

Our Information Officer under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is Nicole Du Toit. Phone 021 572 7777, email nicole@renco.za.net.

3. What we collect

When you use our contact form: your name, company (if you give it), email address and/or phone number, and your message.

When you phone us: your name, number and what you need.

To stop spam, our website host Cloudflare checks your IP address and browser signals when you send the form. We don't keep your IP address.

4. Why, and our lawful basis

To answer your enquiry and, if you ask, to prepare a quote. We rely on POPIA section 11(1)(b) (steps you asked us to take before a possible contract) and section 11(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in replying to messages sent to us). Giving your details is voluntary, but we can't reply without a way to contact you.

5. Who receives it

Cloudflare, Inc. (our website host, which runs the contact form and its spam check) and Resend (the email service that delivers your message to our inbox). Our email provider, [EMAIL HOST], stores it in our inbox. We don't sell your information or use it for marketing.

6. Outside South Africa

Cloudflare and Resend process your message in the United States. We have accepted their data processing agreements, which require them to keep your information secure and to use it only to provide their service to us. POPIA section 72 allows a transfer like this when the recipient is bound by an agreement that gives adequate protection.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. Resend deletes its copy within 30 days. If your enquiry leads to a sale, quotes and invoices are kept for 5 years, as the Tax Administration Act requires.

8. How we keep it safe

Your message travels over an encrypted connection. It isn't stored on our website. Only our staff can open our inbox, and our accounts use two-factor authentication. We use a spam check and rate limits to block abuse.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact Nicole Du Toit on 021 572 7777 or nicole@renco.za.net.

10. Complaints

Please talk to us first. You may also complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, 010 023 5200.

11. Cookies

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. See our cookie policy.
