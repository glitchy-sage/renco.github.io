Power and protection

Getting power into a building safely, keeping it steady, and measuring it.

  • Distribution and protection

    Protect people, cables and equipment in distribution boards.

    Includes: circuit breakers (miniature, moulded case and air), earth leakage units, isolators, fuses, surge protection (also for solar PV), power supplies and control transformers.

  • Power quality and backup

    Switch to a generator when the supply fails, keep backup batteries charged, and correct power factor on larger sites.

    Includes: generator and automatic changeover (ATS) controllers, battery chargers, power factor correction, harmonic filters.

  • Metering

    Measure how much power a building, board or machine uses, and show voltage and current on a panel.

    Includes: energy and multifunction meters, power monitoring, digital and analogue panel meters, current transformers.

Motors and machines

Starting, running and automating motors, pumps, fans and production lines.

  • Motor control and motors

    Start, stop, protect and control the speed of motors on pumps, fans, compressors and conveyors.

    Includes: contactors, direct-on-line and soft starters, variable speed drives, changeover switches, pump panels, electric motors and motor accessories.

  • Automation

    Run machines and processes automatically, and give operators a screen to control them.

    Includes: PLCs (programmable controllers), touch screens, industrial panel PCs, servo drives, motors and gearboxes, encoders.

  • Industrial networking

    Connect machines, panels and sensors to each other and to a network, over cable or mobile (LTE).

    Includes: unmanaged and managed switches, industrial routers, LTE and ethernet gateways, edge gateways.

Control, switching and sensing

The buttons, relays and sensors that tell a machine when to start, stop or warn someone.

  • Control and signalling

    Let operators start and stop machines, and see or hear what a machine is doing.

    Includes: pushbuttons, selector and cam switches, indicator lights, pendant stations, joysticks, signal towers, beacons, sirens and hooters.

  • Timers and relays

    Switch things on and off at the right time, or when something happens, like a tank filling up or a fault.

    Includes: PCB, miniature and solid-state relays, relay modules, modular contactors and timers, monitoring relays, level controls, daylight and time switches.

  • Sensors, switches and safety

    Detect objects, pressure, flow, level and temperature, and stop a machine when someone gets too close.

    Includes: proximity, photoelectric and ultrasonic sensors, pressure and flow switches, temperature controllers, limit and float switches, safety switches, light curtains, lockout hasps.

Boards, wiring and installation

Everything to house, wire, connect and light an installation, and the tools to do the job.

  • Enclosures and climate control

    House electrical gear safely, and keep panels cool and dry.

    Includes: distribution boards (surface and flush), floor-standing boards, plastic, steel and stainless steel enclosures, busbar accessories, panel fans, cabinet air conditioners.

  • Terminals and wiring accessories

    Wire up boards and panels neatly and securely, with every cable labelled.

    Includes: screw and push-in terminals, DIN rail, slotted trunking, ferrules, crimp lugs, cable ties, glands and conduit, junction boxes, heat shrink, cable markers.

  • Plugs and sockets

    Connect equipment to power on sites, in workshops, outdoors and at home, and connect solar panels and batteries.

    Includes: industrial and weatherproof plugs and sockets, socket distribution boxes, household switches and sockets, multi-pole, solar PV and battery connectors.

  • Lighting

    Light warehouses, workshops, offices, outside areas and escape routes.

    Includes: LED floodlights, bulkheads, LED battens, downlights, emergency lighting, lamps and tubes.

  • Tools and testers

    Install, test and find faults safely.

    Includes: cable strippers, crimping tools, cable cutters, hydraulic tools, DIN rail and trunking cutters, multimeters and clamp meters, installation testers, thermal cameras.
