Who we are

Renco Electrical CC is a Cape Town business, established in 2006. Today we do one thing: find the electrical parts and components our customers need, and get them delivered.

We’re a small team, so you deal with one person, Nicole Du Toit, from your first question to delivery. There’s no call centre and no counter to queue at: call, email or send us a message and we’ll get back to you.

Established 2006.

What we do

We supply trade and business customers across South Africa: electricians, contractors, refrigeration and air-conditioning firms, and maintenance teams.

  • Parts and components

    Motor control, circuit protection, control and signalling, metering, enclosures, terminals and wiring accessories, tools and testers, and lighting.

  • Quotes on request

    Send us your list or a part number. We confirm availability, price and delivery cost in writing before you commit to anything.

  • Delivered nationwide

    We don’t have a shop or collection counter. Everything is delivered by courier, anywhere in South Africa.
